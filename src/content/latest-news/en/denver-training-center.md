Northwind has opened its first Mountain West training center in Denver. The
facility includes live installation bays for each product line and a classroom for
certification courses.

> **"Contractors learn faster with a real unit in front of them," said Jamie Rivera, Head of Training at Northwind.**

#### Courses This Fall

Installation and service courses run monthly. Registration opens on the training
portal.
