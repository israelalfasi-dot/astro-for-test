#### **Noise Is a Comfort Metric**

Sleep quality depends on steady, low background sound. A compressor cycling on and
off at 45 dB wakes light sleepers even when the temperature is perfect.

> **The quietest room is the one where guests never think about the HVAC at all.**

#### **What Changes in a Renovation**

- **Inverter compressors:** ramp smoothly instead of switching on and off.
- **Low-speed fans:** most of the night runs below 25 dB.
- **Isolated mounting:** keeps vibration out of the wall.
