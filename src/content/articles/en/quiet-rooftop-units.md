Rooftop equipment is usually an afterthought — a cluster of grey boxes nobody
designed. The new Northwind line starts from the opposite premise: if it will be
seen from the street, it should look like part of the building.

A modular panel system wraps the unit in materials the architect specifies, so the
roof line reads as architecture rather than machinery.
